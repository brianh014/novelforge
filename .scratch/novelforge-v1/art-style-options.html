NovelForge Art Style Picker

Three ways to show an example of each Art Style on step 1 of world creation, then what a title baked into the cover means for each place the cover appears.

The pictures here are stand-ins I drew in code, with colours borrowed from each style. No image has been generated yet. They show layout only, so judge the layout and how much room a thumbnail gets, never the art.

Every mock works: pick a style in any of them and all three follow. The mocks are the right page of step 1 at 640px, the width on a 1366px screen, and a 360px phone. The list and descriptions are the real ones.

A. Thumbnail grid (my pick)

Each style in the list becomes a small picture with its name under it. You can compare all 16 at once with no extra click. It is the smallest change, and the images can be the smallest, but each one is only about 140px wide on desktop and fine detail won't show. The grid is taller than today's list, so short screens scroll the page.

B. List with one large preview

Today's list stays as it is, and one large picture above it shows the selected style. This is the best view of a single style, but you see one at a time, so comparing means clicking through. On a phone the list is 17 rows long and the preview scrolls away, so picking a low row hides what it changed.

C. Chosen style with a full sheet of plates

The page stays calm and shows only the chosen style. A button opens a sheet of larger plates covering the page, like the list of plates in an old book. It keeps step 1 short and gives the thumbnails more room, but every change of style costs an extra click, and the choice no longer sits beside the rest of the form.

Choices to make

  1. Picker layout: A, B or C, or a mix.I suggest A. Size per option: about 320px wide images for A, about 25–40 KB each, so 0.6 MB in total. B and C want about 640px, roughly 1.5 MB. These sizes are estimates until real images exist.
  2. Static or live examples.I suggest static: generated once and bundled, so players pay nothing. Live would charge every player for 16 images.
  3. Sample subject, the same in all 16.I suggest (a): a lone traveller on a hilltop road at dusk, looking toward a distant town, as the stand-ins show. It has a figure, a landscape and light, and fits any theme. Others: (b) a head-and-shoulders portrait of a hooded traveller, which suits anime and comic styles but says less about light and space. (c) a half-open door in an old wall with light behind it, which is striking but has no figure.
  4. Load Game row: L1, L2 or L3.I suggest L1.
  5. Go-ahead for the paid images, and how to run them.3 test images first, in contrasting styles, then 16 once you like the tests: 19 paid images plus any retries, on google/gemini-nano-banana-2.1. Your key stays out of my hands if I write a one-off script and you run it yourself with the ! prefix. The other way is a dev-only route in the running app. I suggest the script. Shrinking would use headless Chrome, so no new dependency.
