World Creation and Settings Layouts

Three ways to take these screens off the two-page spread. Each mockup is drawn at true size, with your real fields and Art Style pictures and a sample story. The line under it is measured from the mockup, and tells you how far you'd have to scroll.

Recommended: C, Chapters. It's the only one where world creation doesn't scroll on a laptop, and the Art Style pictures get a page of their own. A is the cheapest to build. You can also mix them, such as C for world creation and B for Settings.

In all three, the palette, type, grain, sparkle button and everything else you've approved stay as they are. Story, Journal, Inventory, Load Game and the Main Menu stay a two-page book. These pages are only mockups, so nothing on them is clickable except the switches.

A. Single Sheet

One centred page on the desk, with the spine gone. Everything runs down a single column, and Generate or Save stays pinned at the bottom.

Good

Simple to follow, and closest to how phones already work. Fields you read together stay together.

Watch out

The longest scroll of the three on a laptop. Art Style sits far below the brief, and Settings becomes one long list.

Build

Smallest: one layout, plus moving the fields into one column.

B. Index Sheet

One wide page with a slim index down the left. In world creation it lists the steps and what you've chosen so far. In Settings it lists the sections, and shows one at a time.

Good

You can always see where you are. Settings gets much shorter, and your choices stay in view while you work.

Watch out

World choices still scroll on a laptop. It's the least book-like, and the index folds down to a strip on phones.

Build

Medium: a new index, plus the running summary of choices.

C. Chapters

One topic to a page. Art Style becomes its own step, so world creation has four steps, and all sixteen pictures show at once. Settings uses tabs across the top.

Good

World creation doesn't scroll at 1366×800. Each page has one job, so it's always clear where one field stops and the next starts.

Watch out

One more step to click through, and you can't see the brief and the Art Style together. Phones still scroll.

Build

Medium: one more wizard step, and tabs in Settings.

The Settings figures show OpenRouter's fields, in the Text section for B and C. Local servers and ComfyUI add more fields, so those can still scroll in any direction.
