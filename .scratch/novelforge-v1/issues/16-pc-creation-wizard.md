# 16: PC creation wizard

**What to build:** A player can create whomever they want as the PC. Their choices are canon, and the world adapts if they conflict with it.

**Blocked by:** 15

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A player can enter name, background, physical traits, skills, starting inventory and other descriptive fields
- [ ] “Pick for me” fills only empty fields
- [ ] Starting inventory is sorted into wearing and carrying
- [ ] A PC who conflicts with the world is accepted, with a non-blocking warning
- [ ] The Narrator updates the World Bible to fit that PC
- [ ] The first Turn starts from the Starting Hook, or as a sandbox if there isn't one
