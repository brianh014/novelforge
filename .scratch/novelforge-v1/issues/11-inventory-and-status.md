# 11: Inventory and status

**What to build:** A player can see what the PC is wearing and carrying, click an item to act with it, and read the PC's status in words.

**Blocked by:** 09

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Clicking an item opens an input asking what the PC does with it
- [ ] That action appears in the chat as a normal Turn, and the world reacts
- [ ] The Narrator is guided to keep inventory realistic for the world
- [ ] Status shows health, injuries and conditions in words, with no numbers
- [ ] Inventory and status match the Snapshot after every Turn and Revert
