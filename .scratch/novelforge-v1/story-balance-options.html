Paper

A. Wider Plate

The left page takes the larger share of the spread, so the picture grows until it nearly fills the page. Condition and Director's Notes sit side by side beneath it.

Good

The biggest picture of the three, and the simplest to build. Art becomes the star of the screen.

Watch out

The story column loses about a quarter of its width, so more scrolling and shorter lines.

B. Character Card

Widths stay the same. Under the picture, a card shows your PC's portrait next to Condition and Director's Notes, so who you're playing is always in view.

Good

Fills the page with useful things and leaves the story untouched. The card stays in the same place from turn to turn.

Watch out

With images off there's no portrait, so the card is text only. A long list of notes would scroll inside the page.

C. Open Notebook

Director's Notes stop being a link and become a ruled page filling the space under the picture, ready to write in. Condition moves up beside the subtitle.

Good

The most book-like, and notes get much easier to reach. It fills the page at any window height.

Watch out

If you rarely use notes, it's still a blank area, only with lines on it.
