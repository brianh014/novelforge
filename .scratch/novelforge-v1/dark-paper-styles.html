What to look for

Paper reads as paper when you can see unevenness in it: fibres, mottling, or light falling across it. The darker a stock gets, the less of that survives, so the very dark options rely more on flecks and lines.

Each option changes the paper, the ink and the accent colour together, and you can mix them. For example, D's lighting with E's colour. The contrast figure is story text against the lightest part of the page, and 7:1 or more is comfortable for long reading. Texture rendering varies a little between screens.
